Privacy Policy

App: Flash Beacon
Developer: Lighthouse Studios
Effective date: 2026-03-05

This Privacy Policy explains how Lighthouse Studios (“we”, “us”, “our”) processes information when you use the Flash Beacon mobile application (the “App”). If you have questions, contact us at support@lighthousestudios.app.

Short version:
  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We use a purchase service provider (RevenueCat) to unlock and restore your purchases.
  • We do not run ads, and we do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or track you across apps and websites for advertising.

1) Who is responsible for your data?

Lighthouse Studios is the controller for information processed through the App. Contact: support@lighthousestudios.app.

2) Information we process

The App’s core feature (displaying a light beacon on your screen) does not require you to provide personal details. However, when you use optional in-app purchases or restore purchases, some information is processed to complete those actions.

2.1 Purchases and subscriptions

  • Purchase information (e.g., what you bought, purchase/transaction status, and restore status).
  • App user identifier used for entitlement management (typically an anonymous identifier generated by the App/SDK).

Apple processes payments and billing. We do not receive your full payment card details.

2.2 Technical and device information

To operate reliably and prevent fraud, our service providers may process limited technical information such as:

  • Device and app identifiers (e.g., app instance identifiers, device type/OS version).
  • IP address (generally used for security and abuse prevention; may be processed by providers).
  • Diagnostics (e.g., basic error logs related to purchase/restore flows, if generated).

2.3 What we do not process

  • No name, profile, or account details are required.
  • No precise location data is collected by the App.
  • No contact list, photos, microphone, camera, or health data is collected by the App.
  • No advertising tracking (we do not use IDFA).

3) How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide App functionality (unlock purchased content, restore purchases, and maintain entitlements).
  • Customer support (respond to requests and investigate purchase/restore issues).
  • Security and fraud prevention (prevent abuse of purchase/restore mechanisms).
  • Compliance (meet legal and contractual obligations related to App distribution and purchases).

4) Service providers and sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run the App and its purchase functionality:

  • RevenueCat, Inc. — used to manage in-app purchase entitlements, validate purchase status, and support restore purchases. RevenueCat may process purchase-related information and limited technical information to provide these services.
  • Apple — processes payments and billing and provides purchase/restore mechanisms through the App Store.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share information for targeted advertising.

5) Legal bases (EEA/UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, we process information under these legal bases:

  • Contract — to provide purchased content and restore purchases.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the App, prevent fraud, and maintain reliable operation (balanced against your rights).
  • Legal obligation — where required by applicable law (e.g., tax/compliance obligations handled by platforms/providers as applicable).

6) Data retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above:

  • Purchase/entitlement records may be retained to support restore purchases and compliance.
  • Support messages are retained as needed to resolve your request and for reasonable record-keeping.

7) International transfers

Service providers (such as RevenueCat and Apple) may process information on servers located outside your country. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses).

8) Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your information. Because the App does not require an account, we may need additional details to locate purchase-related records (e.g., transaction context). To exercise your rights, contact us at support@lighthousestudios.app.

If you are in the EEA/UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9) Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the “Effective date” above and publish the new version at this URL.
